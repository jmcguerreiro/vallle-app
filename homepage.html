How it works

As easy as buying a coffee

No accounts, no apps, no passwords. Just walk in and ask.

1

Pick a local shop

Walk into any participating store and ask for a Vallle postcard. Choose whatever amount you like.

2

Make it yours

The owner writes the vallle details on the left of the postcard. You add your personal message on the right. Done — ready to give.

3

They use it when they want

The person you gave it to visits the store and uses their postcard. All at once, or a little at a time.

Questions

Good question

It's a beautiful branded postcard that doubles as a gift vallle. On the back, the store owner handwrites the vallle details on the left — amount, unique code, shop name — and there's space on the right for you to write a personal message. One card, gift and vallle together.

Just walk into any participating store and ask. You choose the amount, they fill in the postcard, you write your message, and you're done. You pay the store directly — cash, card, however they normally take payment.

They take the postcard to the store and present it at checkout. The store looks up the code and deducts the amount. They can use the full value in one go, or spread it across multiple visits — whatever suits them.

No problem. The remaining balance stays on the vallle. They can come back and use the rest whenever they like, until the vallle expires.

Each store sets its own expiry period — typically one year, but it can be up to five. The expiry date is written on the postcard and tracked in the system.

Yes — just enter the code from your postcard in the "Check your vallle" section above. You'll see the remaining balance and expiry date instantly.

A gift that actually supports the places you love

Every Vallle postcard puts money directly into a local business — the bakery on your corner, the wine shop you always recommend, the cafe where everyone knows your name.

On the back, the store owner handwrites the vallle details on the left — the amount, a unique code, their shop name. On the right, there's space for your personal message. Gift and vallle, side by side, on one card.

It's not a generic gift card from a faceless brand. It's something from a place you chose, for a person you know, that keeps your money in the neighbourhood.
